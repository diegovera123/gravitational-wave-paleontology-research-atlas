
# Single-Variable Calculus

## Prerequisites
- Algebra
- Functions
- Trigonometry

## Learning Objectives

By completing this unit, learners should be able to:

1. Explain derivatives as instantaneous rates of change.
2. Calculate derivatives using differentiation rules.
3. Explain integrals as accumulated quantities.
4. Apply differentiation and integration to physical motion.

## Research Applications

- Position, velocity, and acceleration
- Orbital motion
- Gravitational-wave frequency evolution

## Mastery Assessment

Derive the velocity and acceleration of a body from its position function.
